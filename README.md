# SPEAKEASY

Learn to think in English


## EVENT OVERVIEW

### What is Speakeasy?

Speakeasy is a social, cultural, and linguistic experience, not a language class.

It is designed for Italians who want to feel more comfortable thinking, speaking, and interacting in English, while remaining fully rooted in their Italian (and local) identity.

Speakeasy is based on a simple idea:

> You don’t learn a language by studying it.
You learn a language by living inside it, even for a short time.



Each event creates a temporary, immersive environment where English becomes the natural language of communication — imperfect, human, playful, and shared.


---

### Core Goals

Speakeasy aims to:

Reduce fear and self-judgment around speaking English

Normalize imperfection and misunderstanding

Encourage participation, not performance

Use culture, media, objects, and conversation instead of textbooks

Show that English is a tool for connection, not an identity replacement

Build confidence through shared experience


English is not taught.
English is used.


---

### Who It’s For

Italians who understand English but don’t feel confident speaking it

People who feel limited or excluded by formal language environments

Sardinians and Italians already navigating multiple languages

Curious minds interested in culture, travel, work, and the world

Anyone who wants to participate more freely in global conversations


No “level” required.
No tests.
No corrections.


---

### THE SPEAKEASY EVENT FORMAT

**1. Arrival & Transition — “The Flight”**

Participants begin in a reception or waiting area.

Italian is still spoken

Airport-style ambience

Lights dim

Italian flight safety announcement

Italian pilot message welcoming passengers onboard

Short airplane engine sound


Then:

English landing announcement (“Welcome to the UK”)

Engine sound stops

Lights rise

EXIT sign illuminates the door into the main venue


Participants symbolically cross a border.

From this point on, English is encouraged throughout the event.


---

**2. Entering the English Space**

Participants enter a comfortable venue designed like an English pub or international lounge.

English background sounds

Informal seating

A small bar

No menus, no signs except: DRINKS


This is the beginning of immersion.


---

**3. Aperitivo Ice Breaker — The English Bar**

Participants order drinks from a barman who speaks only English.

No pointing

No menus

Real interaction


The barman may deliberately “misunderstand” using a playful Joker card — even when English is correct — to normalize miscommunication.

If a participant doesn’t understand, they must turn to another participant and ask:

“What did he say?”

Everyone is encouraged to help each other — in English.

Laughter is expected.


---

**4. “Pack a Bag” — Gifts from Home**

Before the event, participants are asked to bring a small rucksack or day bag containing:

Three small “gifts from home” (Italy).

These can be:

Objects

Food

Photos

Personal items

Symbols of local or national culture


In small groups, participants take turns:

Taking out one object

Explaining what it is

Sharing why they chose it


The objects become conversation starters.

This reinforces an essential idea:

> Learning English is not about leaving your culture behind.
It’s about carrying it with you — and sharing it.




---

**5. Shared Content Moment**

Each event includes a short shared stimulus, such as:

A video clip

A radio segment

A short film

A song

A live or recorded message from an “amico all’estero”


The content is:

Short

Authentic

Discussed together


Conversation happens naturally in small groups.


---

**6. Open Conversation & Connection**

Participants mix freely.

Facilitators guide gently with prompts, but the focus is on:

spontaneous conversation

listening

expressing opinions

asking questions


English is used as much as possible, without pressure.


---

**7. Closing Moment**

The evening closes with:

A short reflection

One takeaway phrase

An invitation to the next Speakeasy


Participants leave not with “new rules,” but with more confidence.


---

### What Speakeasy Is — and Is Not

Speakeasy is:

Immersive

Social

Imperfect

Human

Cultural


Speakeasy is not:

A class

A test

A performance

A place to be corrected

A place to feel judged



---

### The Long-Term Vision

Speakeasy starts as an in-person experience and grows into:

Regular local events

Online participation

A digital archive of voices and stories

A global conversation involving Italians everywhere
